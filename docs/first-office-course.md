# AI for Office Work — Course Overview



**Track:** Role Track — Office / Admin
**Prerequisite:** Fundamentals (F1–F4) or Office Quick-Start
**Estimated time:** 4–8 weeks at 15–20 min/day
**Tier:** Premium



---



## What This Course Covers



This track teaches office and administrative professionals how to use AI in their day-to-day work. Every lesson is grounded in tasks you already do — email, documents, scheduling, data, and meetings — so you see immediate, practical value.



By the end of this course you will be able to:



- Draft, rewrite, and summarize professional emails and documents using AI
- Use AI to prepare for, run, and follow up on meetings more efficiently
- Automate repetitive data tasks with AI assistance
- Build a personal library of prompts for your most common work tasks
- Know when to trust AI output and when to verify it

## Notes for Content Authors



- All examples should use realistic but fictional names, companies, and scenarios
- Avoid referencing specific AI tools in O1–O4; keep prompts tool-agnostic
- O5-4 is the one lesson where specific tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot for Microsoft 365) are named and compared
- Every "Spot the problem" lesson must have a clear, unambiguous answer — no trick questions
- "Mini-task" lessons must have a self-report completion option for users who can't access the tool at that moment
